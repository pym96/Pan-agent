# Distrust-driven verification

- Type: verified-learning-fact
- Verification: triangulated
- Source: user feedback recorded in `HF-20260818-019`, [goal-driven](../sources/2026-08-17-goal-driven-repo.md), [Agent DX CLI scale](../sources/2026-08-17-agent-dx-cli-scale.md), and [Harness Engineering lecture](../sources/2026-08-17-harness-engineering-lecture.md)
- Updated: 2026-08-18

## Verified facts

- The user's accepted process treats upstream completion claims as untrusted until a separate Regulator inspects primary Evidence and applies Criteria.
- Goal-driven independently encodes repeated verification of subagent claims against Criteria.
- Agent DX treats the agent as an untrusted operator and recommends defense-in-depth at the tool boundary.
- The inspected lecture distinguishes strict verification from emotional pressure and reports that hostile framing can worsen model behavior.

## Boundaries

- These sources support distrust of Claims and operators, not hostility toward people or models.
- Deterministic Tests are strong Evidence but are not the only Evidence source and do not remove the need for an Acceptance Gate.
- The exact independence and risk-tier rules are governance decisions outside the Wiki.
