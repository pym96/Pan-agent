# Composio 30-task agent comparison methodology

- Type: verified-learning-fact
- Verification: source-located
- Source: <https://x.com/composio/status/2087889898208367036> and the same-author continuation posts ending at <https://x.com/composio/status/2087889911638569165>
- Updated: 2026-08-18

## Verified facts

- The thread describes a comparison of two models on 30 practical operations tasks executed through Pi Agent, which it characterizes as a minimal third-party harness.
- Disclosed task examples include spreadsheet and Airtable batch edits, Gmail cleanup, GitHub audits, and cross-application ledger synchronization.
- The thread reports pass count, average task duration, Token use, tool calls, cost per task, and cost per successful task.
- Its cost-per-success comparison divides resource cost by successful outcomes rather than treating the lowest per-attempt price as automatically best.
- The thread says cost was estimated from official model API pricing and, for one provider with time-varying prices, used a 50/50 peak/off-peak blend.

## Boundaries

- The thread does not publish the complete 30 tasks, fixtures, runner, graders, raw attempt records, repetition count, variance, or failure taxonomy.
- The reported model numbers are source claims from one thread; they were not reproduced locally and are not evidence about Workspace Agent Harness.
- The task examples span several applications and do not define this project's Vertical Domain Pack Interface.
- The source can guide campaign shape and metrics, but cannot serve as a reproducible benchmark artifact without additional primary materials.
