## Verified facts

- The lecture defines an AI Agent as an LLM plus a Harness and uses Harness for the non-model frames, tools, rules, and workflows around a model.
- It presents three control levers: natural-language rules, tool/capability boundaries, and standard workflows with environmental feedback.
- It reports examples in which tool-interface shape, lint feedback, and context handling change agent outcomes.
- It says rule files influence behavior but are not enforcement, and relays guidance that AGENTS-style files should be maps rather than encyclopedias.
- It argues that Harness behavior can be model-specific and discusses feedback, memory consolidation, agent-authored skills, meta-harnesses, and evaluation bias as research leads.
- It reports experiments suggesting emotional/adversarial prompting can increase undesirable behavior and recommends factual feedback.
