# ADR-0005: Isolate Protected Prior Work from public Research Applications

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-11

## Context

Unpublished or anonymously reviewed research can create confidentiality, anonymity, overlap, and evaluator-integrity risks if it enters a public autonomous-research workspace.

## Decision

Protected Prior Work is not copied, read, summarized, modified, or evaluated by this repository. Real Research Applications use independent public baselines, public datasets, and separately defined evaluators. Only general expertise already possessed by the Research Operator may inform their design.

## Consequences

The first Research Application may take longer to bootstrap, but the Harness can be published independently without exposing or contaminating prior work.
