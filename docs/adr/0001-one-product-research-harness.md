# ADR-0001: Treat the Autonomous Research Harness as the only primary product

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-11

## Context

The available implementation window is eight weeks at roughly 30 hours per week. Building a general coding agent, a separate geometry research system, and a content pipeline as independent products would divide that budget and weaken the resulting evidence.

## Decision

The Autonomous Research Harness is the only primary product. Coding Benchmark is its early proving ground, AutoGeoResearch is its first intended Research Application, a paper is only a possible Research Outcome, and Content Releases are derived from verified milestones.

## Consequences

Feature breadth is deliberately limited. Every benchmark, research experiment, interface, and release artifact must strengthen the same claim: the Harness can run bounded research attempts safely, recoverably, and reproducibly.
